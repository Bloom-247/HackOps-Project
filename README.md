# LendSure AI

### AI-Powered Trust & Risk Intelligence for Informal Lending

LendSure AI helps individuals, families, communities, and small businesses make safer and more informed lending decisions.

Instead of simply saying **"Approve"** or **"Reject,"** LendSure AI analyzes borrower information, financial history, repayment behavior, document signals, transaction patterns, and trust-network data to provide an **explainable lending recommendation backed by evidence.**

> **Lend with confidence. Decide with evidence.**

---

## 🚀 What Does It Do?

LendSure AI takes borrower data and produces:

- 📊 **Repayment Risk** — Low / Medium / High
- 🚨 **Fraud Risk** — Low / Medium / High
- 🤝 **Trust Intelligence Score** — 0–100
- 💰 **Recommended Loan Amount**
- 📈 **Recommended Interest Rate**
- 🗓️ **Recommended Loan Duration**
- 🧮 **Estimated Monthly Payment**
- 🔍 **Explainable Risk Factors**
- 📄 **Evidence Behind Decisions**
- 🕘 **Auditable Decision History**

The goal is not to replace human judgment, but to give lenders better information before they lend.

## 🎯 Problem

Informal lending is common between:

- Friends
- Families
- Local businesses
- Communities
- Small entrepreneurs

But these decisions are often based on incomplete information or personal judgment.

This can lead to:

- Loan defaults
- Fraud
- Hidden financial risk
- Payment disputes
- Poor lending decisions
- Broken relationships

Traditional credit scoring can also exclude people who have limited formal credit history.

LendSure AI addresses this by combining multiple sources of borrower evidence into one transparent lending-intelligence platform.

## 🧠 Core Features
### 1. Borrower Risk Analysis
Analyze one borrower using financial, behavioral, and repayment information.
The system evaluates factors such as:
- Monthly income
- Monthly expenses
- Existing debt
- Requested loan amount
- Employment stability
- Previous loans
- Repayment history
- Late payments
- Income consistency
- Transaction behavior
- Bounced payments

### 2. Six-Month Financial Analysis

LendSure AI analyzes monthly historical information instead of relying only on a single financial snapshot.

The dataset contains six months of:

- Income
- Expenses
- Debt
- Transaction activity
- Bounced payments

This allows the system to identify trends such as:

```text
Income ↑
Expenses →
Debt ↓
Transactions ↑
```
These trends provide additional context for risk analysis.

### 3. Document Intelligence

The platform supports analysis of:

- Identify Documents
- Bank Documents
- Salary slips
- Income Documents
- Buisness Documents

The intended workflow is:

```text
Upload Document 
        ↓
OCR / Extraction 
        ↓
Information Matching 
        ↓
Consistency Checks 
        ↓
Document Quality Analysis 
        ↓
Verification Result
```

Possible results:
- ✅ Verified
- ⚠️ Needs Review
- 🚨 Suspicious

### 4. Fraud Intelligence
The system identifies potential suspicious signals using:
- Identity verification
- Bank statement verification
- Income document verification
- Document quality
- Transaction variance
- Income consistency
- Disputed transactions
- Bounced payments
- Financial inconsistencies

The system reports potential fraud signals rather than making unsupported accusations.

### 5. Trust Intelligence
The platform calculates a Trust Intelligence Score from 0–100 using evidence such as:
- Verification status
- Repayment history
- Financial consistency
- Trust-network information
- Disputed transactions
- Fraud signals

The score is intended as a decision-support signal, not an official credit score.

### 6. Explainable AI
LendSure AI does not simply output:
> **Risk: HIGH**
It explains why.
Example:
```text
Repayment Risk: MEDIUM
Positive Factors
✓ Strong previous repayment history
✓ Stable income
✓ Consistent financial activity
Risk Factors
⚠ Existing debt is relatively high
⚠ Requested amount is high compared with income
⚠ Recent bounced payment detected
```
Each major factor can be linked to the underlying borrower data.

### 7. Personalized Loan Recommendations
After analyzing the borrower, the system can recommend:
```text
Recommended Amount: ₹35,000
Interest Rate: 12% p.a.
Duration: 3 months
Estimated Monthly Payment: ₹X
Decision: APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS
```
The recommendation is generated by the lending decision layer using risk, fraud, trust, and repayment-capacity signals.

### 8. What-If Loan Simulator
Lenders can change:
- Loan amount
- Interest rate
- Duration
The system dynamically shows how the proposed terms affect:
- Monthly payment
- Repayment burden
- Risk
- Recommendation
- Lending decision
Example:
```text
₹30,000 → Lower Risk
₹50,000 → Medium Risk
₹80,000 → Higher Risk
```
This helps lenders understand the consequences of changing loan terms.

## 📊 Dataset
The project uses a synthetic borrower dataset.
Dataset size:
```text
1,500 borrowers
51 input features
```
The dataset contains input features only.
It does not contain pre-generated answers such as:
```text
❌ Repayment Risk
❌ Fraud Risk
❌ Trust Score
❌ Recommended Amount
❌ Recommended Interest Rate
❌ Recommended Duration
❌ Approval Decision
❌ Confidence Score
```
These are generated by the application's analysis and decision layers.
